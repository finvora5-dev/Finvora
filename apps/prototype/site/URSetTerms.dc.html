1. What Finvora is

Finvora is a learning app. It explains money and markets. It does not give financial advice and does not tell you what to buy or sell.

2. Your account

You sign in with your email and a one-time code. Keep access to your email safe.

3. Plus plans

Paid plans renew until you cancel. If you cancel, Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

4. The simulator

The simulator uses virtual money and made-up companies. Nothing in it is a real trade.
