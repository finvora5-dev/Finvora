Finvora launch motion

Four ways the logo can open the app. Each plays once on the app's navy when Finvora is opened from the home screen, then gives way to the first screen. Tap a stage or its Replay button to watch it again.

Where it plays

Only when the app opens, never between screens. Screen-to-screen changes stay as fast as they are now.

Reduced motion

People who turn on reduced motion get a plain fade whichever option is chosen.

What is fixed

The mark, the wordmark and the colours are the existing Finvora ones. Only the movement differs.
